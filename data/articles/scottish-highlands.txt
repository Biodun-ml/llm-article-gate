# Scottish Highlands Road Trip: Five Days from Inverness

This loop starts and ends in Inverness and covers about 600 kilometres of single-track roads, so give yourself more driving time than the map suggests.

Day 1: pick up the car in Inverness and drive west along Loch Ness. Stop at Urquhart Castle for an hour, then continue to Eilean Donan Castle, which sits on a tiny island joined to the shore by a stone bridge.

Day 2 and 3: cross the bridge to the Isle of Skye. Hike to the Old Man of Storr early in the morning, when the car park still has space. The Fairy Pools near Glenbrittle are an easy walk and the water is clear enough to see the bottom from the bank. Portree is the best base, with the widest choice of guesthouses.

Day 4: take the ferry from Armadale to Mallaig, then drive to Fort William. If you have the energy, book a guided hike up Ben Nevis; otherwise, ride the gondola on Aonach Mòr.

Day 5: drive south through Glencoe, the most dramatic valley in the Highlands, before heading back north to Inverness.

Book accommodation on Skye months ahead for summer; it sells out.
