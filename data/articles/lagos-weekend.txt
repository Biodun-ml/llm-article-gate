# A Weekend in Lagos for First-Time Visitors

Lagos, Nigeria, is loud, fast and generous, and a weekend is enough to fall for it if you plan around the traffic.

Saturday morning, go to the Lekki Conservation Centre before the heat builds. The canopy walkway is one of the longest in Africa and the boardwalk through the wetland takes about an hour. Wear proper shoes; the planks can be slippery.

After lunch, drive to Nike Art Gallery in Lekki, a five-storey house filled with thousands of works by Nigerian artists. Entry is free and the founder often greets visitors herself.

In the evening, head to Freedom Park on Lagos Island, a former colonial prison turned into a venue for concerts, food stalls and open-air theatre.

On Sunday, take a boat from the Tarzan jetty in Ikoyi to Tarkwa Bay, a sheltered beach where you can rent a chair and eat grilled fish. Agree on the return time with the boat operator before you leave.

Where to stay: Victoria Island or Ikoyi keep you close to most sights. Getting around: ride-hailing apps work well, but leave early because a 10-kilometre trip can take an hour on Friday evenings.
