# Cappadocia in Two Days: Balloons, Valleys and Underground Cities

Cappadocia in central Turkey is built around one early alarm: the hot air balloon flight at sunrise. Book it for your first morning so that a weather cancellation can be moved to the second.

Most visitors stay in Göreme, a village of cave hotels carved into the soft rock. The Göreme Open-Air Museum, a cluster of monastery churches with painted ceilings, is a ten-minute walk from the centre. Pay the extra ticket for the Dark Church; its frescoes are the best preserved.

In the afternoon, hike through Love Valley, named for its tall rock pillars, and climb to the top of Uçhisar Castle for sunset over the whole region.

On day two, rent a car or join a group tour to Derinkuyu Underground City, which goes down eight levels and once sheltered thousands of people. It is narrow and low in places, so skip it if you dislike tight spaces.

Finish with a pottery workshop in Avanos, a riverside town known for red clay ceramics.

Getting there: fly into Kayseri or Nevşehir; most hotels arrange transfers.
