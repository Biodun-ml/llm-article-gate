# Smoky Party Jollof Rice

This is the jollof my aunt makes for every birthday in Ibadan, scaled down for a family of six.

Ingredients: 4 cups long-grain parboiled rice, 6 plum tomatoes, 3 red bell peppers, 2 scotch bonnet peppers, 2 onions, half a cup of vegetable oil, 3 tablespoons tomato paste, 2 bay leaves, 1 teaspoon dried thyme, 1 teaspoon curry powder, 2 stock cubes, and 3 cups of chicken stock.

Blend the tomatoes, bell peppers, scotch bonnets and one onion until smooth. Fry the second onion, sliced, in the oil until soft, then add the tomato paste and cook for five minutes until it darkens.

Pour in the blended pepper mix and cook it down for about thirty minutes, stirring now and then, until the oil rises to the top. Add the stock, spices and bay leaves.

Wash the rice and stir it in. Cover tightly with foil and then the lid, and cook on low heat for thirty minutes without stirring.

The smoky flavour comes from the bottom layer catching slightly. Turn the heat up for the last three minutes and let it happen.
