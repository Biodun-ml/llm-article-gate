# Cartagena, Colombia: What to Do in Three Days

Cartagena on Colombia's Caribbean coast is hot, colourful and easy to walk, as long as you rest through the middle of the day.

Spend your first morning inside the Walled City, the colonial centre ringed by stone fortifications. Walk the top of the walls at sunset; the stretch near Café del Mar gets crowded, so go further along.

Cross into Getsemaní, the neighbourhood just outside the walls, for street art and cheaper food. A free walking tour leaves from the Plaza de la Trinidad every afternoon.

Castillo San Felipe de Barajas, the largest Spanish fortress in the Americas, is a fifteen-minute taxi from the centre. Go when it opens at 8 to avoid the heat inside the tunnels.

On day three, take a boat trip to the Rosario Islands, a coral archipelago about an hour offshore. Choose a tour that stops at a single island rather than four; you will get more time in the water for snorkelling.

Where to stay: Getsemaní for atmosphere, the Walled City for convenience. Getting there: direct flights from Bogotá take about ninety minutes.
