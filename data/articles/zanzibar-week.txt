# A Week in Zanzibar Beyond the Beach Resorts

Zanzibar is usually sold as a beach holiday, but the island has enough history and wildlife to fill a week.

Begin in Stone Town, the old trading quarter on the west coast of Unguja, Tanzania. Get lost in the alleys, look for the carved wooden doors, and join a spice tour to one of the farms outside town. In the evening, the food stalls at Forodhani Gardens set up along the waterfront.

From Stone Town, a short boat trip takes you to Prison Island, where giant tortoises roam the old quarantine station.

Halfway through the week, visit Jozani Forest, the only national park on the island and home to the red colobus monkey. Guides are required and are included in the entrance ticket.

Finish in Nungwi in the far north, where the tides are gentle enough to swim at any hour. Dhow sunset cruises leave from the beach every evening. For snorkelling, book a day trip to Mnemba Atoll, a protected reef off the northeast coast with turtles and dolphins.

Getting there: ferries from Dar es Salaam take about two hours, or fly directly to Abeid Amani Karume airport.
