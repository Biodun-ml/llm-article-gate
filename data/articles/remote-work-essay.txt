# What a Year of Working Remotely Taught Me About Rest

Last year I worked from four countries and finished it more tired than when I started.

The problem was not the travel itself. It was that every new apartment came with a week of setting up: finding a desk that did not hurt my back, learning which café had reliable internet, and adjusting my calls to a new time zone. By the time I felt settled, it was time to move again.

What helped was treating rest as part of the schedule rather than whatever was left over. I blocked two afternoons a week with no calls. I stopped saying yes to meetings that started before 9 in my local time. I kept the same morning routine in every city so that at least one part of the day felt familiar.

I also learned that I am not someone who wants to see everything. Some months I barely left my neighbourhood, and those were the months I did my best work.

This year I am staying in one place for longer. It is less exciting to write about, but I sleep better.
