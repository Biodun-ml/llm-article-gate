# Ljubljana to Lake Bled and Bohinj in Four Days

Slovenia is small enough that you can base yourself in one place and still see the Julian Alps without long drives.

Spend the first day in Ljubljana. The old town along the Ljubljanica river is compact, and the funicular up to Ljubljana Castle takes under a minute. Book a walking tour if you want the history behind the bridges.

Buses to Lake Bled leave Ljubljana every hour and take about seventy minutes. Rent a rowing boat to reach the island church, then walk up to Bled Castle for the view across the water. Most guesthouses around the lake include breakfast.

The next morning, hike through Vintgar Gorge on the wooden walkways above the river. Arrive before 9 or you will be shuffling behind other visitors the whole way.

On the last day take the bus further into the mountains to Lake Bohinj, which is larger, quieter and colder than Bled. From the western end of the lake, a trail climbs to Savica Waterfall in about twenty minutes. Kayaking on Bohinj is allowed and rentals are by the bridge in Ribčev Laz.

When to go: June and September have warm days without the August crowds.
