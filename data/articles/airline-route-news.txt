# Carrier Announces New Nonstop Route Between Lagos and Lisbon

A European carrier said on Tuesday that it will start nonstop flights between Lagos and Lisbon in March, the first direct link between Nigeria and Portugal in more than a decade.

The route will run three times a week on a narrow-body aircraft with business and economy cabins. The airline said demand had grown among students and small-business owners connecting onward to other European cities.

Industry analysts said the announcement reflects a wider push by European airlines into West African markets, where passenger numbers have recovered faster than expected since 2023. Fares were not disclosed, and the schedule still requires final approval from regulators in both countries.

A spokesperson for the Nigerian aviation authority said the application was under review and that bilateral air service agreements allowed for additional frequencies.

The airline also said it would review its cargo capacity on the route later in the year.
